# Sudha Nunna - Professional Portfolio

A **classic, corporate, and recruiter-ready** portfolio website for a Full Stack Developer, designed to meet the standards of MNCs, product companies, and senior engineering teams.

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

This portfolio follows **strict corporate design principles**:

- **Classic & Timeless**: Clean, professional aesthetic that won't go out of style
- **Corporate Standard**: Suitable for Fortune 500 companies and enterprise environments  
- **Recruiter-Optimized**: Scannable in under 2 minutes with clear information hierarchy
- **Senior-Level**: Demonstrates technical expertise and professional maturity

## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette (Strict)
```css
/* Professional Dark Theme - Single Palette Only */
Primary Background: #0F172A (Dark Navy)
Secondary Background: #111827
Card Background: #020617
Primary Text: #F8FAFC
Secondary Text: #94A3B8
Accent / CTA: #38BDF8
Borders / Dividers: #1F2937
```

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter (Semi-bold) - Professional hierarchy
- **Body Text**: Inter (Regular) - Excellent readability
- **No decorative fonts** - Clean, corporate standard

### Spacing System
- **Consistent 8px/16px/24px grid** - Professional alignment
- **Generous whitespace** - Clean, uncluttered layout
- **Straight lines & soft borders** - Corporate aesthetic

## 🏗 Architecture

### Layout Structure
1. **Hero Section** - Left-aligned, professional introduction
2. **About Section** - Concise expertise summary
3. **Skills Section** - Clean table-style categorization
4. **Projects Section** - SaaS case study format
5. **Experience Section** - Impact-driven timeline
6. **Education Section** - Clean vertical layout
7. **Contact Section** - Minimal with simple form
8. **Footer** - Essential information only

### Content Strategy
- **Professional tone** - Confident, factual, resume-aligned
- **Impact-driven** - Quantified achievements and results
- **Technical depth** - Demonstrates senior-level expertise
- **Concise delivery** - Respects recruiter time constraints
